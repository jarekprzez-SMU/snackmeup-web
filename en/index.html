Air Fryer

Crispy, straight from the basket.

For when the air fryer sits on the counter and you want more out of it than fries. Pick what we're making: a main, a side, a snack or dessert. I'll give you the temperature and basket time, and split the food into as many batches as your basket holds. Two baskets? I'll time them so both finish in the same minute. And tell me about a dish you usually bake in the oven, and I'll convert it for the air fryer.

Meal Prep Premium

A week of meals planned, portioned and scheduled.

Tell me who you're cooking for and on which days. I build the plan, work out the portions, split the shopping and cooking into stages, and walk you through each one.

1

Configurator

You decide what, when and for whom.

Pick the days and the meals, from breakfast to supper. Tick the household members from your profile you're cooking for and add guests. Everyone has their own portion size. A slider sets the character of the plan, from one big batch to lots of variety. You see straight away how many dishes, missions and hours of cooking that means.

  • Days and meals
  • Household and guests
  • Portion per person
  • Plan character
  • Plan diet
2

Draft

You look through the draft and change what you like.

The plan appears as a draft you can browse even before I've picked the last dishes. Any dish can be swapped with one tap: I pick another one based on your hint, or you choose one from your favourites. Each dish shows how many days it stays fresh and what goes to the freezer. You approve only when everything suits you.

  • Pick another
  • Choose from favourites
  • Freshness
  • Freezer
3

Portions and variations

Everyone eats what they can and what they like.

Every meal shows who is eating it and in what portion, along with calories and macros. One tap opts a person out or back in. If someone at home doesn't eat one of the ingredients, garlic for example, they get their own variation of the same dish: one portion with garlic, one without. The cook plan accounts for it while you cook.

  • Who's eating
  • Portions
  • Variation without an ingredient
  • Calories and macros
4

Missions and shopping

Cooking and shopping spread across the week, so you eat fresh.

The plan spreads itself across the days you picked. I work out when to cook so every dish is as fresh as possible, and when to do the shopping. Each mission is one day in the kitchen: you know up front how long it takes and how many containers you'll need, and each one comes with its own shopping list, grouped the way a shop is laid out.

  • Missions
  • Cooking time
  • Containers
  • Shopping list per mission
5

Cook plan

In the kitchen I walk you through it step by step.

The cook plan starts with the setup: which stations and equipment you'll need, how many containers, and the ingredients for the whole mission. Then it goes step by step, with quantities for each step, tips and timing. The steps for all the dishes are arranged together, so you measure and chop once rather than dish by dish.

  • Stations
  • Ingredients per mission
  • Steps with quantities
  • Tips
6

Eating and the summary

You tick off meals and see how the week went.

In the “What I eat when” view every day has its meals to tick off. For each meal you mark who ate and who's skipping this time, and their portion waits in the fridge. When the week is closed you get a summary: how many planned meals made it to the plate, the calories and macros each person ate, and how you rated the dishes.

  • What I eat when
  • Who ate
  • Dish ratings
  • Weekly summary

Questions

Before you download.

Is Snack Me Up free?

Yes. The free plan has a monthly recipe limit, and the app's home screen shows the current limit. Premium removes the monthly limit and adds Meal Prep, dish photos, the full catalogue and cooking history.

I have a food allergy. Can I rely on the recipes?

The allergies and diet in your profile apply to every recipe I put together for you. I can't see what's inside a specific product from the shop, so always read the labels yourself.

Will I be able to buy the ingredients in my country?

At the start you tell me the region where you shop. Dishes can come from any cuisine in the world, but I pick ingredients you can get where you live.

How do I cancel Premium?

Any time, in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. You keep Premium until the end of the period you've paid for. Details: Terms, sections 4 and 5.

What languages is the app in?

The app works in English and Polish.

Will the app connect to my air fryer?

It doesn't need to. I give you the temperature, the time and the number of batches, and you set them on your own machine, so the app works with any air fryer. Just pick your basket size.

From the author

Why I built Snack Me Up

After a full day of making decisions at work, “what are we eating tonight?” was the one decision too many. Recipe apps didn't help. They gave me a thousand recipes to choose from, which is just more deciding.

I wanted something that works like a chef who is also a friend: knows my taste and simply tells me what to make tonight. Every recipe is personalised to my taste and my preferences, because we all like to eat a little differently and to tweak things our own way. When I feel like a different cuisine, a new flavour or something more challenging, I change my preferences in the profile and my personal chef takes it into account straight away. And because it also knows what we've cooked lately, it makes sure there's always something new on the plate.

My day job is data, and I build Snack Me Up in the evenings, for more than six months now. I still have plenty of ideas for new features and improvements, so the app will keep growing. If you try it, share your opinion: tell me what you like and what you'd add or improve.

Jarek Przezdziecki
