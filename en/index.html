How it works

You answer a few questions. I take care of the rest.

1

Step 1 · only at the start

You tell me about your kitchen.

At the start I ask a few questions: allergies and diet, the equipment you have, your budget and where you shop. In your profile you can also add favourite cuisines and ingredients you dislike. You can change any answer whenever you like.

  • Allergies and diet
  • Equipment
  • Budget
  • Where you shop
  • Favourite cuisines
  • Disliked ingredients
2

Step 2 · every time

You tell me what you feel like today.

Type or say a craving, for example “spicy shrimp, 20 minutes”. Or take a photo of your fridge and I'll build a recipe from what you have. And when you have no idea at all, just leave it to me.

  • Type it
  • Say it
  • Photo of your fridge
  • Leave it to me
3

Step 3 · a few seconds later

You get one recipe, made for you.

Instead of a list of a hundred results you get one suggestion, with the cooking time, calories and macros. If you like it, I walk you through the cooking step by step and start a timer where it's needed. One tap adds the ingredients to your shopping list.

  • Cooking time
  • Calories and macros
  • Step by step
  • Timers
  • Shopping list

Inside

Take a look inside before you download.

Before I suggest anything, I check three things.

Can you eat it?

The allergies and diet in your profile apply to every recipe I put together for you. I can't see what's inside a specific product from the shop, so read labels as you always do.

Can you buy the ingredients where you live?

I know where you shop. The dish can come from any cuisine in the world, but I pick ingredients you can get where you live.

Will it be something new?

I know what's been cooked lately and make sure the same dish doesn't come back twice in the same week. The full cooking history is there to browse in Premium.

From the author

Why I built Snack Me Up

After a full day of making decisions at work, “what are we eating tonight?” was the one decision too many. Recipe apps didn't help. They gave me a thousand recipes to choose from, which is just more deciding.

I wanted something that works like a chef who is also a friend: knows my taste and simply tells me what to make tonight. Every recipe is personalised to my taste and my preferences, because we all like to eat a little differently and to tweak things our own way. When I feel like a different cuisine, a new flavour or something more challenging, I change one setting and the next suggestions follow. And because my chef knows what we've cooked lately, it makes sure there's always something new on the plate.

My day job is data, and I build Snack Me Up in the evenings, for more than six months now. I still have plenty of ideas for new features and improvements, so the app will keep growing. If you try it, share your opinion: tell me what you like and what you'd add or improve.

Jarek Przezdziecki
