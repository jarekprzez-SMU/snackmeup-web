How it works

Three steps. The first one you do once.

AI Chef, Kitchen Hero and Sweet Tooth are free within a monthly recipe limit. Meal Prep is part of Premium. Details below

Before I suggest anything, I check three things.

Can you eat it.

Allergies and diet apply to every recipe I put together for you. I can't see what's inside a specific product, so read the labels as you normally would.

Can you buy it nearby.

I know where you shop. The cuisine can be from anywhere, the ingredients have to be on sale in your area.

Have you just had it.

The same dish won't show up twice in a week. The full cooking history, to look back through, is part of Premium.

Free and Premium

What's free and what's Premium.

Free

  • AI Chef with Craving and Random, Kitchen Hero and Sweet Tooth
  • a monthly recipe limit the home screen shows the current limit
  • recipes without dish photos
  • a catalogue of recent recipes only

Premium

  • no monthly recipe limit
  • Meal Prep a plan for several days with a shopping list
  • dish photos
  • the full catalogue and cooking history
